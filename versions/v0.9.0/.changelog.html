Features

  • In game chat, so the players can chat with each other. Activate it with the "Enter" key.
  • Killing dragon gives all allies a buff, called Dragon's Might.
  • level up sound
  • Local chat bubble
  • New hero Besiktaren.
  • New hero FöretaGAREN.
  • New hero Gamer Dad
  • New hero The Dentist
  • Recall to base. Press b to teleport you back to base after a few seconds.
  • Show mana bar and name of player on hero.
  • Special hero interactions
  • Stats panel. Press tab to show stats of all players.
  • Support pings. Holding alt and clicking on the map, shows a ping to the allies.
